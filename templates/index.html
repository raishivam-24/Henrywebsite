Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Yes. We use settings suited to Indian (darker) skin types. Laser gives long-term reduction, typically 70–90% after a full course of 6–8 sessions. One or two top-up sessions a year keep the results.

Melasma can be controlled very well, but it can come back with sun exposure, hormones or heat. Treatment combines creams, peels and sometimes laser, along with daily sunscreen. Regular follow-ups help keep it under control.

Most acne scars can be improved by 50–80% with subcision, microneedling or fractional laser. Deep scars usually need a combination of these. We treat active acne first, then the scars.

Most treatments involve only mild discomfort. Numbing cream is used where needed. Peels and lasers usually have little or no downtime, apart from 1–3 days of redness or flaking. Surgical procedures need a few days of rest. See each treatment page for details.

PRP works best for early to moderate thinning. It strengthens existing hair and reduces hair fall over 4–6 monthly sessions. For bald areas, a hair transplant may be the better option. We check the cause of hair loss first, such as thyroid, iron levels or stress.

When given by a trained dermatologist, they are safe and approved. We aim for a natural, refreshed look, not a frozen one. Results last about 4–6 months for Botox and 9–18 months for fillers.

No treatment changes your natural skin colour permanently. Treatments can reduce tanning and pigmentation and even out skin tone. Steroid-based “fairness” creams bought over the counter can damage skin. Please show us any cream before using it.
